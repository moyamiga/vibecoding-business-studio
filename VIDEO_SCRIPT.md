# Demo Video Script

Target length: 2:30 to 2:50.

Tone: clear, practical, education-focused. Do not frame this as a parent/child
story. Frame it as a repeatable learning method for schools, clubs, and private
project teams.

## 0:00 - 0:15 Hook

Voiceover:

"This is VibeCoding Business Studio: an AI-assisted learning model where
students learn to code, think like product owners, understand business, and work
in teams by shipping a real prototype."

On screen:

- Show the project title.
- Show the TempleFall start screen.
- Show one quick moment of gameplay.

## 0:15 - 0:40 The Problem

Voiceover:

"Most coding education starts with syntax. But real builders need more than
syntax. They need to decide what to build, scope it, test it, explain it, work
with teammates, and understand why a product would matter to users."

On screen:

- Show progress notes or a simple slide with:
  - product decisions;
  - business;
  - teamwork;
  - code;
  - shipping.

## 0:40 - 1:15 The Method

Voiceover:

"Our method turns a project into a small product studio. The learner acts as the
CEO or product owner. Codex acts as the build partner and coach. The team defines
roles, documents responsibilities, builds in small steps, and connects technical
choices to real business questions like customers, costs, marketing, pricing,
and retention."

On screen:

- Show the method diagram or a simple list:
  - idea;
  - scope;
  - build;
  - test;
  - business lesson;
  - team decision.

## 1:15 - 1:55 Product Demo

Voiceover:

"The current demo project is TempleFall, a mobile-first battle royale prototype.
It includes a jungle start screen, tap movement, bots, loot, health, shield,
ammo, relics, temple objectives, a secret reward loop, and a shrinking vegetation
zone. The game is intentionally small: the lesson is to turn a big idea into a
working vertical slice."

On screen:

- Open the prototype.
- Tap Start.
- Move the player.
- Pick up loot.
- Show bots.
- Show the HUD.
- Show temple objectives or secret-room loop if possible.

## 1:55 - 2:25 How Codex Was Used

Voiceover:

"Codex helped transform raw ideas into structured requirements, implement the
prototype in TypeScript and Phaser, debug mobile rendering issues, draft the
game design, and prepare the project for submission. But the key idea is that
Codex accelerates execution while the learner keeps ownership of the product
decisions."

On screen:

- Show code briefly.
- Show build running successfully.
- Show a documentation page or project notes.

## 2:25 - 2:50 Why It Matters

Voiceover:

"VibeCoding Business Studio can be used in private projects, school clubs, and
entrepreneurship classes. Students do not just learn to prompt an AI. They learn
to ship, explain, coordinate, and think like builders."

On screen:

- Show closing title:
  "VibeCoding Business Studio"
  "AI-assisted coding + business + teamwork"
  "Education Track"

## Recording Checklist

- Keep the video under 3 minutes.
- Use English narration or English subtitles.
- Do not show private messages, tokens, phone numbers, or personal data.
- Do not use copyrighted music.
- Keep the screen readable on YouTube.
- Show the project actually running.
- Mention Codex and GPT clearly.
- Mention the Education track.
